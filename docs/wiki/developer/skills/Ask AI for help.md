# 向AI求助
> wiki 版本：`0.0.8`.0

遇到问题时，**先尝试获取信息，再寻求他人帮助**。

获取信息的方式有很多：
- 🌐浏览器搜索
- 🤖询问 AI
- 🙋请教有经验的人
- 🙏在社交平台公开求助

### 🔎先搜索，再提问

在请教他人前，不妨先自行搜索：
- 提问前做功课，能让你的**提问包含更多关键信息**
- 很多常见问题都有人遇到过，可能有现成的解决方案
> 当你询问他人不了解的内容时，对方同样需要先搜索相关信息😓

### ♿不知道怎么开始？直接问 AI

如果你不知道怎么描述问题，**直接用自己的话发送给 AI 即可**。

AI 可以帮你：
- 理清问题并提炼关键词
- 联网搜索并查找相关资料
- 给出可能的解决方向

> ⚠️ 注意：AI 的回答不一定准确，请将其作为整理思路与获取信息的辅助工具，而非绝对标准答案。

### 💡不要一个人苦思冥想

有的时候，你并不是不知道该怎么搜索，而是**没有意识到自己应该去搜索了**。

不要让自己一直困在原地，**主动向外获取信息**，往往比独自闭门苦想更有效。

# English
> wiki version: `0.0.8`.0

When you run into a problem, **try gathering info first before asking for help**.

There are many ways to get information:
- 🌐 Search in browser
- 🤖 Ask AI
- 🙋 Pick the brain of someone experienced
- 🙏 Post your question on social media

### 🔎Search first, ask later

Before asking others, try searching on your own first:
- Doing a little homework helps you **include more key details in your question**
- Odds are someone else has already solved your issue
> If you ask people about something they don't know, they'll just end up searching for it anyway😓

### ♿Don't know where to start? Just Ask AI

If you don't know how to word your problem, **just type it out to AI in your own words**.

AI can help you:
- Sort out your thoughts and pull out key points
- Search the web and pull up useful material
- Give you a few ideas on where to start

> ⚠️ Note: AI isn't always right. Use it to organize your thoughts and gather info, not as the final answer.

### 💡Don't try to figure it out all alone

Sometimes it's not that you don't know how to search, but **it just hasn't clicked that you should search**.

Don't stay stuck in place. **Reaching out for info** is usually way better than staying stuck in your head.
